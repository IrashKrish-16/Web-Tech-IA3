How Adoption Works

Taking home your new companion in three stress-free steps.

01

Find Your Match

Browse our verified list of pets. Read through their personalities, care needs, and energy levels.

02

Submit an Inquiry

Click "Adopt Me" to fill out a fast, free adoption application form telling us about your living space.

03

Meet & Welcome Home

Our shelter team will review your form, schedule a fun meet-and-greet, and help you bring your pet home!

Why Adopt From PawsomeMatch?

When you adopt, you are saving two lives: the animal you welcome into your family, and the next rescue pet who takes their spot in the shelter.

  • Fully vaccinated & dewormed
  • Microchipped & spayed / neutered
  • Free initial wellness checkup with a partner vet
  • Post-adoption support and transition guidance
